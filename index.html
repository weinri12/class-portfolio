Platt is currently a student and student employee with MSUIT Central Network Operations at Michigan State University.

About

I troubleshoot and repair network complications all across the MSU East Lansing campus. Solving complex problems is what I enjoy most. I'm currently a fulltime MSU student pursuing a bachelor's degree in Information Science. I also work as a firearms instructor for the Michigan State Police at the MSP Training Academy.

My previous occupations included Michigan State Police Trooper, Military Police platoon sergeant in the United States Army, firearms instructor trainer, weapons armorer, and armed security officer.

I am passionate about solving problems and working with people to find effective solutions. Teamwork is essential in every project and the success of the team depends on the collaboration and communication among its members.

Projects

MSU Breslin Center

I was directly involved with and responsible for the setup and deployment of new IT networking devices required for improved communications throughout the facility. The capabilities of these devices have greatly enhanced the overall network performance and reliability for staff and visitors of the Breslin Center.

MSU Spartan Power Outage Response

The power outage of 2026 affected the entire campus, and I volunteered to come in after hours to help restore the internet connections responsible for maintaining communication across the campus. The affected areas were quickly identified and prioritized for restoration.

State of Michigan Pistol Registration

As a member of the Michigan State Police, I greatly contributed to the development of the MiPistol electronic firearm registration website for the State of Michigan. This program has significantly improved the efficiency and accuracy of the firearm registration processes across the state. The project allowed the state to move away from a paper only copy of firearm registration and move all records to a digital format.

Michigan Forfeited Weapons

The redesign of the Confiscated Firearms Disposition.xlsx TD-006, turn-in and destruction process for all firearms and other weapons seized by Michigan law enforcement agencies was a project I designed and completed. This improvement afforded the State of Michigan the ability to streamline their operations and improve accountability with regards to the destruction of forfeited weapons.

Military Deployments

My service to this country called me away for two deployments in defense of the nation and it's citizens. The deployments instilled the importance of teamwork, adaptability, problem solving under pressure, and risk management/response.
